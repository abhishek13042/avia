# AVIA web surfaces

## Scope and mode

Whole site. Home is Persuade. Upload and run result are Operate. Benchmarks and about are Read. Header, footer and tokens are shared. Backend, data and routes stay as they are.

## Audience and job

A resume reviewer with a few minutes, often on a phone, deciding whether the work is real; a grower or curious visitor trying one clip. They must see a tracked result in seconds, reach the upload in one move, and be able to check how every number was measured.

## Chosen direction

The stencilled apple box end, assigned by the seed and kept. Assumed without a decision round; the owner can overrule it.

Weighing: Swiss poster wall competitive (holds product clarity, loses audience identification). Depot destination blind declined. Green phosphor terminal declined. Bebop title card, mesophotic dive and orizuru crane were not carded.

Raise, from the Swiss poster wall: one colour means current. On paper, red appears only on what is selected or happening now: the playhead, the active step, the current row. It is never decoration.

Raise, from the depot blind: every state change is one physical step. The count moves in whole line-heights and nothing fades in. A failed step stops where it is and says why, it does not reset.

## Unresolved

Public URL, hosting and sign-in are the owner's. Mask HOTA is pending and shows as pending.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every apple box ever shipped carried its count on the end. AVIA is the machine that earns that number from video, so the page is a box end and the count is the stamp.

OWN-WORLD: Fruit-trade box ends and pasted crate labels: chromolithograph ink blue ground, straw yellow letterpress, apple red, stencilled grade and count. Label paper for working pages. No wood texture, no vintage kitsch, no fake distress, no drawn fruit; the only apples are the real tracked ones.

STORY: See the count earned on real footage, drop a clip, learn why adding frames lies, compare measured scores beside the published baselines, read the limits, go.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full ink blue label. The real tracked clip sits in the label's picture window, playing. A straw stencil plate on the window's corner rolls the count upward in step with the video. Heading and working upload share the viewport. If someone left now they would describe a number climbing as apples get outlined.

FORM: Stencil display, Franklin text with tabular numerals, square corners, ruled frames, ink on paper. Signature interaction: the count roller, synced to playhead on home and run pages. SEED REFERENCE: seed:4047d7ce38ce5938.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
